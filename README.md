## How to Make a Vendor Order on the Self Plan App

This guide will walk you through how to make a vendor order on the platform. The process is seamless and even as a new user, this guide should help you complete your order without hassle.

1. **Log in or Create an Account**
Open the Self Plan App.
Sign in to your account or create a new account.

2. **Go to "Create Event"**
Enter your event details:
Event type
Date
Location
Expected number of guests
Budget

3. **Go to the "Vendors" Section**
Select all the services you need, such as:
Catering
Decoration
Photography
MC
DJ/Live Band
Makeup
Rentals
You can use filters such as location, price, rating, availability, and service type to find the right vendors for your event.

4. **View a Vendor's Profile and Select a Service or Package**
View the vendor's profile and select a service or package that suits your needs.
Choose the package that fits your event.
Specify the quantity where necessary. For example, 100 guests for catering or 10 tables for rentals.

5. **Enter Your Event Details**
Provide the information the vendor needs, such as:
Event date and time
Venue
Number of guests
Contact information
Proposed budget
Additional requests

6. **Click "Place Order"**
After placing your order, the vendor can either accept or reject it.
If the vendor accepts your order, proceed to make the payment.

7. **Click "Make Payment"**
Complete the payment for your order.
After payment, you should receive an order or booking confirmation.

8. **Set Up a Consultation Call**
Once your order has been confirmed, click "Set Up Consultation Call" to schedule a call with the vendor and discuss your event details.

You can also go to My Orders, Bookings, or My Events to view your order status.
